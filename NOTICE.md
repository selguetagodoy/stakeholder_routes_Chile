# NOTICE

Stakeholder Routes Chile is an independent analytical dataset authored by Sebastián Elgueta Godoy.

## Public-source evidence

The repository maps regulatory and investment pathways using official legal, regulatory and institutional sources. Statutes, regulations, public consultations, institutional descriptions and official records remain attributable to their original publishers.

The route model, actor-stage structure, conditionality framework, decision-point model, monitoring architecture and analytical organization are project work.

Public availability does **not** imply a single license over all upstream legal or institutional material.

## Citation

Use `CITATION.cff`, `CITATION.bib` or the latest confirmed version DOI for the dataset and preserve source attribution for substantive legal or institutional evidence.
