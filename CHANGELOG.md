# Changelog

## v0.3.0 — 2026-09-19

- Added a dated snapshot of 13 current authorities for institutions represented in the route model.
- Added 7 regulatory/public consultations with open/closed status as of 2026-09-19.
- Added 8 regulatory-calendar milestones, preserving whether dates are official exact dates or derived from an official duration.
- Kept volatile monitoring data separate from stable institutional and route architecture.
- Added a dedicated public monitoring view and validation rules.

## v0.2.0 — 2026-09-19

- Expanded coverage from 14 to 22 routes and from 45 to 72 actor-stage records.
- Added sanitary authorization for food installations, archaeological intervention, maritime concessions, MOP public-works concessions, native-forest management plans, aquaculture technical approval, SERNAC PVC and Environmental Sectoral Permits (PAS).
- Expanded to 21 policy-competence mappings and 17 explicit decision points.
- Added `actor_index.csv` with 49 actors and `route_dependencies.csv` with 16 conditional relationships.
- Preserved a strict distinction between route overlap, conditional activation and mandatory legal sequencing.

## v0.1.0 — 2026-09-19

- Created 14 stakeholder and decision routes for Chile.
- Added 45 actor-stage records with mandatory/conditional/optional flags.
- Added 13 policy-competence mappings and 9 explicit decision points.
- Added a primary-source ledger and validation workflow.
- Added a composite data-center route without representing it as a universal legal sequence.
- Documented the deferred 2026-12-01 entry into force of Law N°21.719 for future data-protection route versioning.
